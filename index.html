Why this rabbit hole exists

Our mission.

We're here to examine conduct, not debate policy.

We're not building a website that tells people what to think about Donald Trump. We're building a website that asks people what they believe in—and then gives them the documented evidence to examine conduct through those values.

We're interested in character, honesty, integrity, compassion, respect, and accountability. You can agree with someone's politics and still examine their conduct. You can disagree with someone's politics and still examine the evidence fairly.

Our evidence promise

We aim to provide original sources wherever possible; relevant dates and context; fair representation of responses and defenses; and clear labels distinguishing allegations, civil findings, criminal convictions, opinions, and unresolved questions. Errors should be corrected transparently.

No assigned conclusions. Read the facts. Explore the context. Decide for yourself.
